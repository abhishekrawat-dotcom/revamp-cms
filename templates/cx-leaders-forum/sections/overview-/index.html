Experience is the Enterprise

Customers today do not experience an organisation in departments. They experience the product. The app. The store. The salesperson. The payment. The delivery. The service call. The refund. The AI agent.

For them, every touchpoint is a CX. Product design is CX. Search is CX. Delivery is CX. The call centre is CX. Payments are CX. Data architecture is CX. Employee behaviour is CX. AI is CX.

To the customer, all of it is one enterprise.
