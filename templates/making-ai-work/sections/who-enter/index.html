The Problem Isn't AI Adoption Anymore. The Enterprise Was Built Around Human Action. Agents Change the Equation.

For decades, enterprise systems have followed a familiar model: People decide. Systems execute. Agentic AI starts to break that model.

Agents can interpret context, make decisions, call systems, trigger workflows and act across the enterprise. That changes more than technology. It raises new questions about decision rights, access, accountability and how work itself is organised.

Four Questions the Enterprise Now Has to Answer

  • Decision Rights

    If an agent can decide, who gives it the authority?

  • System Access

    What happens when agents can act across the systems that run the business?

  • Accountability

    When an autonomous action creates an outcome, who owns the result?

  • Operating Model

    If work can move at machine speed, can organisations built around human speed keep up?
