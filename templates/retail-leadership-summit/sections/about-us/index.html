About ETRetail

ETRetail is the retail news vertical of The Economic Times. It offers in-depth news, analysis, views, interviews, podcasts and global trends in the retail sector. ETRetail also enables industry leaders to come together on a single platform and discuss the latest developments in the country's burgeoning retail market. Our coverage includes trends, innovation, technology, FDI, mergers & acquisitions, policy and launches in the retail and consumer sector.

ETRetail also sends out a daily retail newsletter to more than 4 lakh of its loyal subscriber base to keep them up to date with all the latest happenings in the retail sector.
