Factoids of the Retail Industry

₹82 Trillion Market Power

India’s retail market reached ₹82T in FY24 and is projected to double by 2034, fuelled by strong consumer demand.

Tier-2/3 Growth Engine

Smaller cities and rural India are driving the next wave, with consumption projected to cross ₹190T+ by 2034.

Massive Economic Contributor

Retail contributes ~10% to India’s GDP and employs over 45 million people.

Retail Real Estate Surge

3.1 million sq. ft. of retail space leased in Q1 2025 alone, with leasing demand up 69% YoY.

Future-Ready Malls

16.6 msf of Grade-A malls in the 2025–26 pipeline, attracting global, luxury and experience-led brands.

Global Brands Betting on India

60+ new international brands entered in 2024, the fastest expansion pace in a decade.

Premium & Experiential Retail Rise

High consumer spend on luxury, premium products and immersive shopping experiences is reshaping India’s retail landscape.
