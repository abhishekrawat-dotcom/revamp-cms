India’s retail sector is at an inflexion point — a trillion-dollar industry still largely unorganised, now being rewritten by kirana stores, mega malls, digital-first D2C brands, q-commerce disruptors and AI-powered marketplaces all at once.

It is this bold evolution that forms the foundation of the summit — the country’s definitive platform for shaping the future of commerce, bringing together every stakeholder in the retail value chain: legacy retailers and D2C founders, mall developers, tech innovators, e-commerce leaders, supply chain disruptors, policymakers and investors.
