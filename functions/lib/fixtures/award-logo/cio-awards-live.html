About the Award

India's Benchmark for Enterprise Technology Excellence

The ET Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence recognise visionary CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, CAIOs, CISOs, technology leaders and enterprises that are leveraging AI, data, cloud, cybersecurity, digital engineering and emerging technologies to drive innovation, business transformation and measurable impact.

With categories spanning AI, Data, Digital Transformation, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Engineering, Enterprise Applications, Manufacturing, Sustainability and Emerging Technologies, the awards recognise the leaders, teams and initiatives redefining modern enterprises. Every category is designed to celebrate measurable business impact, innovation and technology excellence across the enterprise.

The Judging Criteria

Strategic Relevance

Alignment with business priorities and significance of the problem/opportunity addressed.

Innovation & Differentiation

Originality of the approach and the extent to which it creates a meaningful competitive advantage.

Technology Excellence

Quality, sophistication and effectiveness of the technology architecture, platforms and solutions deployed.

Scale & Implementation

Breadth, depth, adoption, execution quality and scalability of the initiative.

Business Impact

Measurable outcomes and tangible contribution to business performance.

Experience & Ecosystem Impact

Impact on customers, employees, partners and the broader enterprise ecosystem.

Future Readiness & Sustainability

Ability to evolve, scale and create a sustainable technology foundation for the future.

Leadership & Benchmark Potential

Strength of leadership, organisational transformation and potential to become an industry benchmark.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need Before You File

01What are the ET Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence 2027?+

India aspires to become a leading digital force, with the goal of achieving a $1 trillion digital economy by 2028. Enterprise technology is the cornerstone of India's digital transformation, fostering innovation and growth. On this journey, visionary digital leaders are crucial in propelling India to new heights. It is time to recognise the outstanding efforts of these leaders who spearhead digital excellence. Following the successful launch of Enterprise IT Excellence Awards, featuring N. R. Narayana Murthy, Founder of Infosys, as the Grand Jury Chair, we are now poised for an impressive third edition — The Economic Times Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence 2027. The Economic Times Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence 2027 will highlight India's business and digital transformation successes. By bringing together India's leading IT minds, the awards will serve as a breeding ground for innovative ideas and strategies.

02What categories are included in the ET Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence 2027?+

The awards include multiple categories that recognise enterprise technology leadership, digital transformation, innovation, cybersecurity, data and analytics, cloud adoption, customer experience, and other high-impact technology initiatives.

03Who can participate in the ET Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence 2027?+

The ET Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence recognise visionary CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, CAIOs, CISOs, technology leaders, and enterprises that are leveraging AI, data, cloud, cybersecurity, digital engineering, and emerging technologies to drive innovation, business transformation, and measurable impact.

04What criteria are used to evaluate nominations for the ET Awards for Enterprise Tech Excellence 2027?+

Nominations are generally evaluated on innovation, strategic relevance, execution quality, scalability, measurable business impact, stakeholder value, and the sustainability of the technology initiative.
